    How to play

    You start with a five-letter word. Each round you keep some of its letters and add one, making a word one letter shorter. Keep going until you are down to a single letter.

    1. 5 → 4 Select three letters in a row. Add one letter in front or behind.
    2. 4 → 3 Select two letters in a row. Add one letter in front or behind.
    3. 3 → 2 Select one letter. Add one letter in front or behind.
    4. 2 → 1 If your two-letter word holds an A or an I, let it fall. Those are the only one-letter words.

    The letters you keep stay side by side and in order. The letter you add goes on one end, never in the middle.

    Every letter you add must be one you have not used yet. Anything already on the board, including the letters of the opening word, is greyed out on the keyboard and cannot be played again.

    Scoring

    • 2 points for every word you make.
    • 1 point for the new letter each word brings in.
    • Undo costs 1 point, once there is a word to take back. Backing out of a split you have not committed is free.

    A clean run to a single letter with no undos is 11 points.
